Questions

Who are these cards for?

Anyone who finds it hard to switch tasks or switch off, especially autistic and ADHD adults who work from home. The language is written with those brains in mind, but nothing in the cards requires a diagnosis.

Is this a physical product?

No. It is a digital PDF that you print at home. Nothing is shipped.

Do I need a special printer?

No. Any home printer works. Double-sided printing is the easiest. Choose to flip on the short or long edge, depending on how your printer turns the page.

Is this medical or therapeutic advice?

No. It is a self-support tool. If things feel heavy, please talk to a doctor or therapist. A few cards use physical techniques such as cold water on the face, so check with your doctor first if you have a heart condition or other health concerns.

Can I share the cards or print them for others?

The kit is for personal use only. Please don’t resell or share the file.
